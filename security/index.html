1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines the information security principles and controls adopted by Wood1 ("we", "us") to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of all data processed in connection with our TikTok Shop integration services.

It applies to all employees, contractors and interns, to all systems, networks and facilities we operate, and to any third party that accesses data within the scope of our services.

2. Governance and Responsibilities

Information security is owned and supported by senior management, who allocate the resources required to maintain and continually improve our security program. Security roles and responsibilities are formally defined.

  • A designated Security Owner is responsible for operating, monitoring and reviewing security controls.
  • A Data Protection Officer (DPO) oversees data protection and privacy compliance.
  • Defined personnel are responsible for secure development, access review and third-party assessment.

Production access authorization and audit records are not controlled by a single individual. High-risk operations — data deletion, product publishing, permission changes — are subject to approval and logging.

3. Security Policy Framework

We maintain a set of formally approved, documented and regularly reviewed policies and procedures, including:

  • Information Security Policy (this document)
  • Access Control Policy
  • Data Classification and Encryption Policy
  • Endpoint and Workplace Security Baseline
  • Incident Response Policy
  • Vulnerability and Threat Management Procedure
  • Personal Data Protection Policy
  • Business Continuity and Backup Procedure

These documents are approved by management, communicated to relevant personnel, reviewed at least annually, and updated after any significant change.

4. Access Control

  • Access is granted strictly on a least-privilege, need-to-know basis using role-based access control (RBAC).
  • Every user has a unique account. Shared accounts and shared credentials are prohibited on production systems, cloud consoles and code repositories.
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is enforced for all administrative and sensitive system access.
  • Strong password requirements and account lockout policies are enforced. Failed login attempts are logged and alerted.
  • Access is provisioned through a formal joiner / mover / leaver process, and is revoked promptly upon role change or termination — on the same day for departures.
  • User access rights are reviewed at least annually; privileged accounts are reviewed at least every six months.
  • Administrative access to production databases and servers is granted only through a controlled bastion host with approval and full session logging.

5. Data Classification and Encryption

Information is classified by sensitivity, and handling, storage and transmission requirements are applied to each level.

Confidential — personal dataSeller contact information, certification documents, API credentialsEncrypted at rest, strict least privilege, no external transfer
Restricted — business dataProducts, SKUs, pricing, inventory, listing attributesEncrypted at rest, role-based access
InternalInternal documents, system logsAccess on a need-to-know basis
PublicPublished policiesNo restrictions
  • Data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or above. Cleartext protocols are disabled.
  • Sensitive data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 or equivalent, covering databases, object storage and backups.
  • Sensitive fields such as phone numbers and identity document numbers are encrypted at rest or masked in display.
  • Encryption keys are stored in a dedicated key / secret management system, separated from application code and configuration files. Keys are rotated at least annually, and immediately upon personnel change or suspected compromise.

6. Network and Infrastructure Security

  • The production environment is logically segregated into network zones (public, application, data tiers), with firewall and security-group rules restricting traffic between them.
  • Development, staging and production environments are separated both logically and by credentials. Production data is not copied into lower environments; testing uses sanitized or synthetic data.
  • Cloud resources follow a hardened configuration baseline: no publicly exposed databases, minimal open ports, encryption and audit logging enabled.
  • Infrastructure administration is restricted, authenticated with MFA and monitored. Default accounts and default credentials are not used.

7. Endpoint and Workplace Security

  • All company endpoints run centrally managed anti-malware with real-time protection, automatic updates and periodic scans.
  • Full-disk encryption, automatic screen lock, strong password and account-lockout policies are enforced.
  • Operating system and application security patches are applied in a timely manner.
  • Production data may not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage, and may not be transmitted through personal email accounts.
  • Remote administrative access is permitted only through controlled channels.

8. Logging, Monitoring and Audit

  • Security-relevant events are logged, including authentication events, permission changes, data access and export, API calls, and system anomalies.
  • Logs are centrally collected, protected against unauthorized modification or deletion, and retained for at least 180 days, or longer where required by law or contract.
  • Alerts are configured for anomalous logins, bulk data access and privilege-escalation attempts, with defined response ownership.

9. Vulnerability and Threat Management

  • Production systems are scanned for vulnerabilities at least quarterly.
  • Remediation targets: critical vulnerabilities within 7 days, high within 30 days, medium within 90 days, or a documented compensating control in the interim.
  • Security testing is performed at least annually, with findings tracked to closure.
  • Emerging vulnerabilities and threat intelligence are assessed for impact on our systems.
  • Scan and test reports are securely retained for audit and compliance review.

10. Secure Development and Credential Protection

  • Software changes undergo code review before merging; critical branches are protected.
  • Development follows a secure development lifecycle with separation of development, testing and production environments and credentials.
  • Input validation is enforced on both client and server sides, including image specifications, mandatory product attributes and description HTML allow-lists.
  • Idempotency keys are used for product creation and modification to prevent duplicate operations.
  • Third-party and open-source components are tracked and upgraded to address known vulnerabilities.
  • API keys, application secrets and platform credentials — including TikTok Shop App Keys and App Secrets — are stored server-side in a secret management system, are never embedded in client-side code, and are never committed to public repositories.

11. Personnel Security

  • Employees and contractors are bound by confidentiality obligations that survive the end of the engagement.
  • Background verification is carried out before hiring, consistent with applicable law and the sensitivity of the role.
  • All personnel receive security awareness training at onboarding and at least annually thereafter, covering phishing, credential hygiene, data handling and incident reporting.
  • Violations of security policy are handled under company disciplinary procedures and may result in legal action.

12. Third-Party and Sub-processor Management

  • Sub-processors are engaged only where necessary to deliver our services, and only after an assessment of their security and privacy practices.
  • Sub-processors are bound by written agreements — including Data Processing Agreements where applicable — requiring them to protect data, to process it only on our documented instructions, and to delete it upon termination.
  • Sub-processors may not use platform or seller data for their own purposes, and may not use it for model training.
  • A current list of sub-processors is maintained, kept consistent with actual system integrations, and provided to the platform or sellers on request. Material changes are assessed before adoption.
  • Third-party security posture is reviewed periodically; services presenting unacceptable risk are suspended or replaced.

13. Data Retention, Deletion and Data Subject Rights

  • Data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the service and to meet legal or contractual obligations.
  • Deletion is triggered by seller deauthorization, a valid deletion request from the seller or the platform, termination of API access, or the end of the contractual relationship.
  • Deletion is completed within 30 days of the trigger, and covers production systems, cached listing metadata, backups and sub-processors. Deletion records are retained for audit purposes.
  • As a service provider, we process personal data only on the documented instructions of the relevant seller or platform, and we assist them in responding to access, correction, deletion and consent-withdrawal requests.
  • Data processed in connection with the service is stored in Chinese mainland. Where data is transferred across borders, we apply technical and organizational safeguards — including encryption in transit and at rest, and least-privilege access controls — so that the data continues to receive an appropriate level of protection.

14. Incident Response and Breach Notification

We maintain a documented Incident Response Policy with clearly assigned roles, responsibilities and escalation paths. Incidents are classified by severity:

SeverityDescriptionResponse target
P1Confirmed data breach or core service outageResponse initiated within 1 hour
P2Suspected intrusion or unauthorized accessResponse initiated within 4 hours
P3General security anomalyTriaged within 1 business day

The process covers detection, reporting, containment, forensic analysis, remediation, recovery and post-incident review. In the event of a confirmed security incident or personal data breach affecting platform data, we will contain and remediate the incident and notify the platform and affected parties without undue delay — ordinarily within 24 hours of confirmation — in accordance with applicable law and our contractual commitments. An incident response exercise is conducted at least annually and documented.

15. Business Continuity and Backup

  • Critical systems and data are backed up daily (incremental) and weekly (full). Backups are encrypted and retained for 30 days.
  • Restoration is tested at least quarterly, with results documented.
  • Documented contingency procedures define recovery objectives (RTO / RPO) and responsible owners for critical service interruptions.
  • Single points of failure in core components — database, storage, key service — are identified and mitigated.

16. Compliance

We comply with the TikTok Developer Policies, the TikTok Shop Open Platform rules, and applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR and the CCPA/CPRA where applicable.

We are not currently certified to ISO/IEC 27001 or SOC 2. Our security program is designed in alignment with the ISO/IEC 27001 framework and we plan to pursue certification. Supporting documentation can be provided to partners and platforms under appropriate confidentiality arrangements.

Certifications held: None
