Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-06  ·  Last updated: 2026-10-06

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Wood1 ("we", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares and deletes information in connection with our software application for TikTok Shop sellers (the "App"). The App helps sellers publish and synchronize product listings, listing attributes, pricing and inventory on TikTok Shop through the official TikTok Shop Open Platform APIs.

This Policy applies to sellers who authorize the App, and to visitors to our website. It should be read together with our Information Security Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We follow strict data-minimization principles and collect only the information necessary to deliver the features described in this Policy.

CategorySpecific dataSource

Product and listing dataCategory and category rules, listing attributes, brand and brand authorization status, product title, description, images, SKUs, pricesTikTok Shop Open Platform APIs and the seller's own input

What we do not collect. We do not collect, process, store or transmit buyer personal information such as name, phone number, email address or shipping address. The App does not access order, financial, settlement, fulfillment or returns data, and we do not request the corresponding API permissions.

3. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate the seller's shop and route requests to the correct authorized shop.
  • To publish products and synchronize listing attributes, pricing and inventory on the seller's behalf.
  • To validate listing data before submission, including mandatory attributes, image specifications and category requirements.
  • To display synchronization status, errors and results in the seller's dashboard.
  • To maintain security, monitor for abuse and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not use seller data for advertising, profiling or any purpose beyond delivering the App's features. We do not sell data.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where required, we process information on the basis of the seller's authorization and our contract with the seller, our legitimate interest in operating and securing the App, and compliance with legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent data. We share information only as necessary to operate the App, and only with service providers bound by written agreements:

6. Data Storage and Retention

Data is stored in secure server environments in Chinese mainland. Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the App and to meet legal or contractual obligations.

On deauthorization or a valid deletion request, all associated data is deleted from production systems, backups and sub-processors within 30 days. Deletion is irreversible.

7. International Transfers

Data processed in connection with the App is stored in Chinese mainland. Where data is transferred across borders, we apply technical and organizational safeguards — including encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256), role-based access control and least-privilege access — so that the data continues to receive an appropriate level of protection.

8. Your Rights

Sellers and their authorized representatives may:

  • Request access to the information we hold about them;
  • Request correction of inaccurate information;
  • Request deletion of their information;
  • Withdraw authorization for the App at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@wood1.cn. As a service provider, we also assist TikTok Shop and sellers in responding to such requests.

9. Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect information, including encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256), role-based access control with least privilege, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, network segregation, logging and monitoring, vulnerability management, and secure development practices. Full details are set out in our Information Security Policy.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is a business tool intended for use by sellers who are at least 18 years old. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above, and where appropriate we will notify sellers through the App or by email.
